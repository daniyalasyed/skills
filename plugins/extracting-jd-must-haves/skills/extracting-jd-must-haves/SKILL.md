# Extracting JD must-haves

Run this on the full, verbatim job description:

Extract the top 3–5 non-generic must-have skills/experiences from the JD. Ignore vague terms like "team player" or "excellent communicator". Ignore the standard requirements and responsibilities for this type of role. Focus on the unique elements that the hiring manager is likely specifically looking for in an ideal candidate.
